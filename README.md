# Opening Range Gym

A free practice site for the 15-minute opening range breakout with same-day (0DTE) options.

**Play it:** https://nickroberts90.github.io/opening-range-gym/

## What's inside
- **Quick Read:** look at the opening range and the 9:45–10:15 window, then call the morning at 10:15: call, put or no trade.
- **Full Trade:** play the morning forward, pick a strike from a live option chain, set stops and targets, scale out.
- **Chart Reps:** 10-question drills on reading candles, levels, indicators, setups and chart patterns, in six levels.
- **Lessons:** the opening range, confirmation closes, VWAP, traps, sweeps, gaps, timing, exits and chop.
- **Odds coach:** after every round, statistics from 30,000 simulated mornings, quoted only where they agree with real market days.

Every ticker, price and option quote is simulated. The generator is tuned to the behavior of real stocks, but no real market data is published here. Your progress is saved in your own browser.

## Not investment advice
This is an educational tool. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell anything, and results in the simulator do not predict real trading results. Options trading carries substantial risk.

## How it's built
One self-contained `index.html` (no build step to run, no server) plus `coach.json`, the odds table. It's hosted for free on GitHub Pages.
